**Supplementary Figure S1:** Gene-set intersections for ZBM, XKC, and the XYT core-herb set. Shared disease genes total 78, 842, and 1,595, respectively. ![](/home/data1/zhiyu/data/Github/Li_Project/Thyroid_TCM/edit.Paper/output/20261025_04_review_support/20261025_Figure5.png)

**Supplementary Figure S2:** Exploratory docking poses of β-sitosterol (CID 222284). (A) Human CYP3A4 (PDB 6UNI); (B) human CaV3.2/CACNA1H (PDB 9AYG). Left panels show the local view of the docked ligand (green sticks) and surrounding residues; right panels show the global receptor view. Poses correspond to the minimum supplementary smina scores of −9.12 and −9.68 kcal/mol, respectively. The poses should be interpreted in light of the receptor-preparation and search-region limitations described in the text.
